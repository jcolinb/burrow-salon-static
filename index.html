I have been a hairstylist for over 20 years. I have lived and worked from coast to coast and in the midwest. I have spent a lot of time investing in my education throughout the years. Learning from top educators with Wella, Bumble & Bumble and Aveda. My goal as your stylist is to help you love your hair. I believe it is important to use products made from organic ingredients that are toxin free and environmentally friendly. Maintaining healthy hair and scalp is an important first step to loving your hair. I have experience with anything from a short pixie, to long textured layers, shags or a french bob. As for color we can do natural dimension, something bold or maybe you are just looking to cover your greys that are coming in. I hope to see you soon!
